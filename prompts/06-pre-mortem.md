# 06 · The Pre-Mortem

> Imagine the venture has already gone hollow or out of hand — and name the
> foundational gap that caused it, while there's still time to fix it.

The future-tense twin of the [Altitude Check](./03-altitude-check.md). Where the
altitude check audits *what is*, the pre-mortem audits *what will bite*. Cheap
insurance: a few minutes of imagined hindsight buys you the scaffolding you're
currently tempted to skip.

- **Fire when:** before committing to a big direction; when momentum is high and the
  temptation to skip groundwork is highest; at milestone planning.
- **Engine:** reasoning over the project — run directly, or alongside
  [`/altitude-check`](../skills/altitude-check.md).
- **Pairs with:** [`03-altitude-check`](./03-altitude-check.md) (present-tense
  sibling) — run them together for a full "now and next" audit.

## The prompt

```
Imagine it's 6 months out and <PROJECT> has started to feel out of hand or
hollow. What foundational gap is the most likely root cause? What
scaffolding should exist NOW that we're tempted to skip? Give me the
single highest-leverage thing to put in place before we proceed.
```

## Variables

- `<PROJECT>` — the effort, and optionally the big decision you're about to make.

## What good output looks like

- A concrete **most-likely root cause**, told as a story from the imagined future.
- The **scaffolding that should exist now** to prevent it.
- A single **highest-leverage action** to take before proceeding — so the prompt
  ends in a decision.

## The failure it prevents

The "out of hand" feeling arriving *after* it's expensive to fix. Pre-mortems move
the realization earlier, when the fix is still a cheap document or a small
refactor — exactly the window this repo's foundation gap was caught in.
