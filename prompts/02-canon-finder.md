# 02 · The Canon Finder

> Locate the tried-and-true foundation a field treats as non-negotiable — and flag
> which parts are gated, before the gates matter.

A zoom-in on the *foundation layers* the [Cartographer](./01-cartographer.md)
surfaced: what are the bedrock resources everyone in this craft has internalized,
and can you actually get to them? Tuned to the recurring annoyance that the best
material is often paywalled, borrow-only, or behind an anti-bot wall (HTTP 403) —
so you find and stash it *while you can*.

- **Fire when:** right after the Cartographer, on the foundation rungs it named;
  or any time you need the "what should I actually read/study" answer.
- **Engine:** research-heavy — `deep-research` skill (or
  [`/canon-finder`](../skills/canon-finder.md)).
- **Pairs with:** [`01-cartographer`](./01-cartographer.md) (gives it the layers to
  search within); its access-flagging mirrors this repo's
  [`VERIFICATION_NOTES.md`](../../research/VERIFICATION_NOTES.md) convention.

## The prompt

```
What are the 5–10 foundational, tried-and-true resources the <DOMAIN>
field treats as non-negotiable canon? For each: what it teaches, why it's
load-bearing, and its access status (freely available / borrow-only /
paywalled). Flag anything gated or likely to be hard to reach later, and
write down enough that I can find it again offline.
```

## Variables

- `<DOMAIN>` — the craft, or a specific foundation layer from the Cartographer's map
  (e.g. "functional harmony" rather than all of "music").

## What good output looks like

- A short, ranked list (5–10) — the *canon*, not a link dump.
- Per item: **what it teaches · why it's load-bearing · access flag**
  (`free` / `borrow-only` / `paywalled` / `403-prone`).
- For gated items, enough detail (title, author, edition, stable identifier) to
  re-find them later, and a note on *where* to reach them.

## The failure it prevents

Two at once: (a) studying the *trendy* sources instead of the *foundational* ones,
and (b) discovering the load-bearing text is unreachable exactly when you need it.
Find the canon and pin down its access **early**.
