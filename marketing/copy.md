# META_THEORY — marketing copy

Ready-to-paste copy for different outlets. House voice: honest, specific,
anti-hype. No "revolutionizing," no "cutting-edge," no "passion for." Say what
the kit does and who it's for, then stop.

The one fact every piece is built on:

> **An LLM answers the question you ask, at the altitude you ask it.** It will not
> volunteer the foundation beneath the answer. So the leverage move is to ask for
> the *curriculum*, not just the answer — once, deliberately, early.

## Show HN

**Title:**
> Show HN: META_THEORY – prompts that map a craft's foundation before you build with AI

**Body:**
> When you build something with an LLM, it answers at the altitude you ask. Ask for
> a working result and you get one — but never the foundation underneath it, the
> part every expert in the field takes for granted. So you end up doing advanced
> work on a base you never learned in order. The gap is invisible until it isn't.
>
> META_THEORY is my fix for that, turned into a reusable kit:
>
> - **Six subject-agnostic prompts** you paste into any chat — *cartographer* (map
>   the full ladder of mastery before building), *canon-finder* (locate the
>   tried-and-true foundation), *roots* (trace the historical lineage),
>   *first-principles*, *altitude-check* (audit: are you above your foundation?),
>   *pre-mortem*.
> - **Claude Code skills** that wrap the highest-value prompts so the discipline
>   runs itself, and can be put on a recurring loop.
> - **A "maestro" spec** for a repo that pays recurring "ontological house-calls"
>   to a whole portfolio.
>
> It's not a prompt-pack for output. It's a checklist for *sequence* — the order
> you learn a craft in, which is most of the expertise. Free and open. Feedback on
> the prompts especially welcome.
